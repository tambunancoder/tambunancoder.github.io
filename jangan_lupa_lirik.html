Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

LX GameSoft ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application Jangan Lupa Lirik (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our App, in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, applicable privacy laws, and third-party advertising requirements.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not directly collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number. However, the App utilizes third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) that automatically collect non-personal and technical data to deliver advertisements, optimize performance, and prevent fraud.

Data automatically collected by third-party services may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device model, operating system version, hardware specifications, and network carrier.
  • Usage Data: In-app interactions, ad viewability metrics, click-through rates, crash reports, and performance analytics.
  • Coarse Location Data: General location inferred from your IP address.

2. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Our App displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks to keep the service free. These partners use advertising identifiers and tracking technologies (such as SDKs or cookies) to serve personalized ads based on your interests and analyze ad performance.

Please review the privacy policies of our integrated advertising partners for details on their data practices and opt-out options:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/
  • Unity Ads: https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Advertising Choices and Opt-Out

You can manage your personalized advertising preferences or reset/limit your Advertising ID directly from your device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and choose to Delete advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking permissions for this App.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). Our advertising SDK partners are configured to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements where applicable. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

5. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not directly store personal data on external servers, retention is governed by our third-party advertising partners in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to ensure secure operation within our App environment.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application updates. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date." You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
